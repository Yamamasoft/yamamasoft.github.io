2Information the game uses

Your progress and settings

The game keeps your progress (the levels you’ve completed, your stars and XP, your coins, gems and boosters, and the rewards, themes and avatars you’ve collected) and your settings, such as sound, music and language.

  • Android: it’s stored on your device. If you’re signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also saved in your Google account through Google Play Games’ Saved Games, so you can carry on from another device.
  • Web: it’s stored through CrazyGames’ Data module: in your CrazyGames account if you’re logged in, or in your browser if you play as a guest.

When the game starts for the first time, it reads your device’s or browser’s language to choose English or Arabic. Your language choice stays on your device.

Signing in with Google Play Games or CrazyGames

You can play the whole game as a guest. Signing in keeps your progress safe and lets you play on more than one device. On Android, buying gems also needs a sign-in.

  • Google Play Games (Android). If your device is signed in to Google Play Games, the game signs in automatically when it starts; you can also sign in from Settings. The game then receives your Play Games player ID, your Play Games name and the web address of your profile picture. It shows your name in the game and uses your account for Saved Games, the leaderboard and purchases.
  • The leaderboard (Android). While you’re signed in, the game posts your XP total to Tap&Pop’s leaderboard on Google Play Games. The leaderboard is public: other players can see your Play Games name and your score. Your Google Play Games settings control how your profile appears.
  • CrazyGames (web). If you’re logged in to CrazyGames, the game receives your CrazyGames username and the web address of your profile picture, and shows your name in the game. To log in, use Settings in the game, which opens CrazyGames’ own log-in window; you log out on CrazyGames itself.

The game doesn’t store your account ID. To know which account a saved game belongs to, it keeps a one-way code (a hash) made from the ID, which can’t be turned back into it.

Daily Rewards and Quests

Daily Rewards and Quests are run by a small server of ours, so that days and rewards are counted fairly on every device. It runs on Unity Gaming Services, a cloud service of Unity Technologies, for both the Android and web versions.

  • An anonymous account. When the game starts, it signs in to the server with an anonymous account: a random player ID created by Unity. Nothing is asked of you. The server doesn’t receive your name, your email address, or your Google Play Games or CrazyGames account; the only exception is the one-way code used to check gem purchases, described below. A new installation of the game, or another device, gets a new anonymous account.
  • What the game sends. Your device’s time zone (so a new day starts at your midnight), an ID for each play session, the matches and level wins you make and when, how far you’ve progressed (your furthest level and the boosters you’ve unlocked), whether ads can be shown, and the result of any ad you choose to watch for a reward.
  • What the server keeps. Your time zone, your Daily Rewards calendar, your Quest progress, the rewards it has given you, and when your recent play sessions started (up to nine days back), to count each action on the right day.
  • Technical information. Like any online service, Unity’s servers also receive technical information, such as your IP address, your device type and operating system, the game’s version, and a random installation ID that Unity’s software creates on your device.

Buying gems (Android)

On Android you can buy gems with real money through Google Play. You need to be signed in to Google Play Games to buy. The web version sells nothing.

  • Google Play takes the payment. We never receive your card or bank details or your billing address.
  • Matching a purchase to your account. When you buy, the game gives Google Play a one-way code made from your Play Games player ID. Google returns it with the purchase, so the purchase can be matched to the account that made it.
  • Crediting your gems. The game sends our server the product you bought, the purchase token Google Play issued and that one-way code. The server asks Google Play to confirm that the purchase is real and belongs to your account, then adds the gems. The one-way code is used only for this check.
  • The purchase record. For each purchase it credits, the server keeps the order number Google Play assigned, a one-way code made from the purchase token (not the token itself), the product, the number of gems, the time and your anonymous player ID. This makes sure a purchase is never credited twice, and lets us help if you contact us about a purchase. Your device also keeps a one-way code of each purchase it has credited.

Ads

Ads keep Tap&Pop free. Some appear between levels. Others are optional: you choose to watch them for a reward, such as more coins after a win, a second chance at a level, or extra Daily Rewards and Quest progress.

  • Android: ads come through Unity LevelPlay, from two ad networks run by Unity: ironSource Ads and Unity Ads.
  • Web: ads come from CrazyGames.

Child-safe ads for every player. We don’t ask anyone’s age, so on Android the game treats every player as a child. Before any ad loads, the game tells LevelPlay that:

  • the app is directed to children, under the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA);
  • no one has agreed to personalised ads, so ads aren’t based on your activity in other apps or websites;
  • your information must not be sold;
  • your device’s advertising ID must not be used; and
  • only ad sources certified under Google Play’s Families policy may serve ads.

On top of that, the Android app doesn’t ask for permission to read the advertising ID, or to use Android’s Privacy Sandbox ad features (Topics and Attribution), so ad networks can’t get them through the game.

What ad networks may still receive. To show an ad and count it, an ad network typically receives technical information such as your IP address (from which a rough location, like your country, can be worked out), your device type, operating system, language and screen size, an identifier its software creates for this app (not your device’s advertising ID), which ads were shown and tapped, and diagnostic data. Ad networks use this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under their own privacy policies.

On the web, CrazyGames chooses and shows the ads and runs its own cookie and consent choices; the game doesn’t send CrazyGames any information for ads. The game also tells CrazyGames when gameplay starts and stops.

What the game doesn’t collect

  • your email address, phone number or postal address;
  • your real name, age, date of birth or gender (the only name the game sees is the one on your Google Play Games or CrazyGames profile);
  • your precise location;
  • your contacts, photos, videos or files, or anything from your camera or microphone;
  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your payment card or bank details.

We don’t use analytics or crash-reporting services. The game has no chat, and players can’t send each other messages or content.

4Children

Tap&Pop is a family-friendly game that people of all ages, including children, may play. Because we don’t ask anyone’s age, every player gets the protections a child should have:

  • the whole game can be played without signing in and without giving us any information;
  • ads are child-safe and never personalised, and the Android app can’t read the advertising ID (see Ads);
  • we never ask for a name, email address, age or other contact details;
  • there is no chat, and no way to share content with other players.

Signing in uses a Google or CrazyGames account, which follows that company’s rules for children’s accounts. Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases, for example with Google Family Link.

If you’re a parent or guardian and have a question, or want us to delete information about your child, write to privacy@tappopgame.com. Uninstalling the game, or no longer playing it, stops all collection.

5How long we keep information

  • On your device or in your browser: until you uninstall the game, clear its data, or clear your browser’s storage.
  • In your Google Play Games account (Saved Games and your leaderboard score): until you delete them (see section 6).
  • In your CrazyGames account: as CrazyGames’ privacy policy describes.
  • On our server: Daily Rewards and Quests records for as long as we run those features; purchase records for as long as we sell gems, so that a purchase can never be credited twice.
  • Ad networks keep information as their own privacy policies describe.

6Deleting your data

You can delete what the game keeps at any time.

  • Everything on your Android device: uninstall Tap&Pop, or open your phone’s Settings, then Apps, Tap&Pop, Storage, and clear its storage (the names vary by phone).
  • Your Google Play Games data for Tap&Pop, such as its saved game and your leaderboard score: in the Google Play Games app, open More, then Settings, then “Delete Play Games account & data”, and under “Delete individual game data” tap Delete next to Tap&Pop. Google explains this in Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.
  • On the web as a guest: clear your browser’s site data for crazygames.com.
  • Your CrazyGames account’s data: see CrazyGames’ privacy policy for how to delete your account and its data.
  • Our server’s records: your Daily Rewards and Quests record uses an anonymous ID that isn’t linked to your name, email or account, so we can’t find it from a request that names you. Once you uninstall the game or clear its data (or your browser’s storage), that anonymous account can’t be used again, and the record can no longer be connected to you. If you’ve bought gems, send us the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with “GPA.”) and we’ll use it to find your records and delete them, except the purchase record we need to keep so the purchase can’t be credited twice.

For any other request, write to privacy@tappopgame.com.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Saudi Arabia’s Personal Data Protection Law, the GDPR in the European Union or the United Kingdom, or a US state’s privacy law), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw any consent you gave;
  • receive it in a format you can take elsewhere; and
  • complain to a data protection authority. In Saudi Arabia, this is the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

To use any of these rights, write to privacy@tappopgame.com. We’ll answer within 30 days. We may ask for details to confirm that a request is yours. Because we keep very little that identifies anyone, we may not be able to link some information to you (see section 6).

Why we may use information. Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on: providing the game and the features you choose to use; our legitimate interests in keeping rewards fair, preventing fraud and funding a free game with child-safe ads; and meeting our legal obligations.

8Where information is processed

Google, Unity and CrazyGames run their services in many countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including in the United States and the European Union. These companies protect such transfers as their privacy policies describe and as the law requires.

9Security

  • The game’s connections to all these services are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Only our server can change its records. The game can read its own record, but can’t change it.
  • Wherever we can, we keep one-way codes instead of account IDs and purchase tokens.
  • Only the team members who run the server can access it.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we hold.
